Let your viewers pay Bits to talk to your AI characters. Setup takes about five minutes.

A Custom Power-up is a button viewers buy with Bits. You make one here, then point it at a character.

Open your Power-ups dashboard:

Click to open {{.DashboardURL}}

Under Custom Power-ups, press + Add New Custom Power-up.

{{else if eq .Step.Number 2}}

Name the Power-up after the character you want it to talk to. Viewers see this name.

Open your characters page ↗

Turn ON "Require Viewer to Enter Text". This is required.

If this stays OFF, the Power-up will not work: viewers can't type a message and your character stays silent.

Set a Cost in Bits. The lowest Twitch allows is 10.

Description, icon, background colour, cooldowns and limits are all optional.

Press Create.

{{else if eq .Step.Number 3}}

Twitch never shows you the Power-up's ID anywhere, so we catch it straight from your chat.

Keep this page open. Go to your channel and redeem the new Power-up once yourself, typing any text you'll recognise.

Redeeming Power-ups on your own channel is free, so the test costs you nothing.

Your redemption appears on the right within a couple of seconds. Copy the ID. You'll paste it in the next step.

{{else}}

With the ID copied, open Characters and pick the character the Power-up is named after.

Press Add Reward on it, then under Use existing reward paste the ID and press Link reward.

Flip the toggle to AI if the character should answer in its own voice, or leave it on TTS to just read the viewer's message out loud.

Redeem the Power-up once more. Your character should answer on stream. You're done.

Nothing happening? Check the Control Panel. A connected redemption shows up there even when the overlay is quiet.

{{end}}
