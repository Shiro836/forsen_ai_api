This guide gives you a personal volume control for the AI, separate from what viewers hear on stream.

Download the Audio Monitor plugin for your OS from GitHub releases, close OBS, install it, then open OBS again.

Requires Control audio via OBS enabled on your BAJ AI browser source — see step 4 of the OBS setup guide.

{{else if eq .Step.Number 2}}

Select the BAJ AI source in the Sources dock.

Press Filters in the toolbar below the preview.

You can also right-click the source and choose Filters.

{{else if eq .Step.Number 3}}

Find the Audio Filters section in the top-left corner, then press the + button under it.

No Audio Filters section? Control audio via OBS is not enabled — see step 4 of the OBS setup guide.

{{else if eq .Step.Number 4}}

Choose Audio Monitor from the list.

Audio Monitor missing from the list? The plugin is not installed — go back to step 1 and restart OBS after installing.

{{else if eq .Step.Number 5}}

Click OK.

{{else}}

Device — where you hear the AI. Pick your headphones, or leave Default.

Volume — your volume only, viewers are not affected by it. What viewers hear is the BAJ AI fader in the OBS Audio Mixer.

Leave Volume linked to source volume unchecked, otherwise your volume follows the mixer fader again.

Done. The Audio Mixer fader sets the viewers' volume, this filter sets yours.
{{end}}
